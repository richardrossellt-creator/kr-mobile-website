Where we work

FAQ

Common questions

Do you come to my home?

Yes. We're a fully mobile detailing company and do every job at your location: home, apartment, condo or office.

Can I bring my car to you?

No. We don't have a shop, drop-off location or waiting area. We always travel to you, by appointment.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. We bring our own water and our own power supply.

Can you work at my apartment building?

Yes, as long as management allows mobile detailing and our service vehicle can park next to your car.

Do I need to be there during the service?

Not necessarily. If we have access to the vehicle and it's arranged in advance, we can complete the detail while you're away.

Which package should I choose?

If you want both the inside and outside cleaned, the Full Detail costs less than booking interior and exterior separately. Then add extras like pet hair removal or odor treatment if you need them.

Will my card be charged when I book?

No. Your card only secures the appointment. Free cancellation or rescheduling is available up to 12 hours before.
