About KR Mobile Car Detailing

KR Mobile Car Detailing provides professional mobile car detailing in Arlington, VA and Northern Virginia. Our goal is to make vehicle care simple by bringing high-quality detailing directly to your home or office.

What we do

Interior, exterior and full detailing, at your location

We specialize in interior detailing, exterior detailing and complete car detailing, helping drivers keep clean, protected and well-maintained vehicles without visiting a shop. We are 100% mobile and bring our own water and power.

  • Book online in minutes. Choose your package and vehicle size and secure your spot. Your card is not charged at booking.
  • We come to you. Homes, apartments, condos, offices and garages where mobile detailing is allowed.
  • Relax while we detail. We handle everything on-site so you can focus on your day.
