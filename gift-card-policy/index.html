Gift Card Policy

KR Mobile Car Detailing LLC

KR Mobile Car Detailing LLC

Last updated: April 2026

1. General Use

Gift cards issued by KR Mobile Car Detailing LLC can be used toward any of our services.

They are valid for mobile detailing services in Arlington, VA and the surrounding DMV area.

2. No Expiration

All gift cards do not expire and can be used at any time.

3. Non-Refundable

Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.

4. Transferability

Gift cards may be purchased and sent as a gift to friends, family, or any third party.

5. Partial Use & Remaining Balance

  • Gift cards do not need to be used in full in a single transaction
  • Any unused balance will remain available for future use

6. Redemption

Gift cards can be redeemed:

  • Online through our booking system
  • In person at the time of service

The customer must provide the gift card code or proof of purchase at the time of redemption.

7. Lost or Stolen Gift Cards

KR Mobile Car Detailing LLC is not responsible for lost, stolen, or unauthorized use of gift cards.

8. Service Booking Required

Use of a gift card is subject to appointment availability.

Customers must schedule a service in advance.

9. Limitations

  • Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash
  • Gift cards cannot be reloaded unless explicitly stated
  • Gift cards may not be combined with certain promotions (if applicable)

10. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to update this Gift Card Policy at any time.

Updates will be posted on this page.
